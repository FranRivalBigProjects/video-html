How it works

  1. Choose or drop one or more video files. They stay on your device the entire time.
  2. Each video is processed in sequence: 3 frames are extracted at 25%, 50%, and 75% of its duration, at full native resolution.
  3. The 3 frames are merged side by side into one contact sheet, keeping their original size, and offered as a JPG download named after the video file.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool upload my video to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using HTML5 video and canvas. Your video files never leave your device.

What is a video contact sheet?

A contact sheet is a single image made of several still frames from a video placed side by side, similar to a striped flag layout, useful for quick previews and content reviews.

Can I process more than one video at a time?

Yes. You can select or drop multiple video files at once and each one is processed in sequence, with its own set of frames and contact sheet.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is free, requires no signup, and does not add watermarks to the extracted images.
