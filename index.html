How it works

  1. Choose or drop any video file. It stays on your device the entire time.
  2. The tool automatically extracts 3 frames at 25%, 50%, and 75% of the video's duration.
  3. The 3 frames are merged into one contact sheet image and offered as a JPG download named after your video file.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool upload my video to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using HTML5 video and canvas. Your video file never leaves your device.

What is a video contact sheet?

A contact sheet is a single image made of several still frames from a video placed side by side, useful for quick previews, storyboards, and content reviews.

What video formats are supported?

Any format your browser can play, typically MP4, WebM, and MOV.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is free, requires no signup, and does not add watermarks to the extracted images.
